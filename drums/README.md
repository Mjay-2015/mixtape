# Acoustic drum kit

Salamander Drumkit by **Alexander Holm**, SFZ mapping by **kinwie**, trimmed samples by
**Jesse Chappell** (essej). Licensed Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported
(CC BY-SA 3.0, full text in LICENSE.txt). Sources: archive.org/details/SalamanderDrumkit and
github.com/endolith/Salamander-Drumkit (commit 9e9c9fca9f26e1763435beca4170505d0fa32fb0), trimmed set
salamanderDrumkitSamplesFixedOnsets.zip (MD5 8080018e91ad940efd884dde65bad207).

Changes made for Mixtape (this modified set is shared under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 licence):
the regions played at the default hi-hat pedal position, at most 3 alternate takes per
velocity layer, mapped to General MIDI drum notes (toms also tuned to fill the GM tom notes),
trimmed to 0.7 to 4.5 seconds with a short fade, scaled by one common gain, 16-bit stereo FLAC.

Music made with this kit is yours: the share-alike condition applies to the samples
themselves, not to songs or scores made with them.
